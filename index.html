        Temple & hill etiquette

        • Shoes off, shoulders covered, and ask before photographing people or deities.
        • Haridwar, Rishikesh and the Braj temple towns are vegetarian and alcohol-free.
        • Never drive hill roads after dark; landslide-prone in monsoon (Jul–Sep).
        • Carry cash — ATMs thin out beyond Rudraprayag and networks drop in valleys.
        • Say “Radhe Radhe” in Braj and “Jai Badri Vishal” in Garhwal.
